The signature moment

Every colour has exactly one match in the room.

It's the whole night in one small object. You're not swiping through profiles or reading a bio — you're looking around a table of twenty people, trying to spot the one wrist that matches yours.

When you find them, you tie the ribbon together in a bow. Simple, a little nerve-wracking, and somehow the best icebreaker we've found — because for a few minutes, everyone at the table is looking for someone.

Before you sign up

Good to know.

Do I have to come alone?

No — in fact you can't. Every guest brings a friend of the opposite sex who's also single, so nobody walks in without a familiar face. You'll still be seated to meet new people.

What if I have dietary restrictions?

Tell us on the sign-up form and the menu will be adjusted for you.

What happens to my photo and info?

It's used only to organise seating and matching for this event, and deleted afterward.

How many people come each time?

We keep it to 20 — enough for a proper mix of people, small enough that no one gets lost in the crowd.
