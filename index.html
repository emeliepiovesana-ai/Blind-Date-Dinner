Before you sign up

Good to know.

Do I have to come alone?

No, you don't have to. Guys can bring a guy friend who's also single and getting set up, and girls can bring a girlfriend. It's an option in the form, so you're both invited to the same Tuesday night dinner.

How does the matching actually work?

Girls are contacted beforehand and told their colour. Guys get an actual ribbon on arrival, in the colour that matches their date. It's on the guys to go find their match by asking around, see "See how it works" above.

What if I have dietary restrictions?

Tell us on the sign-up form and the menu will be adjusted for you.

What happens to my photo and info?

It's used only to organise the evening and matching, and deleted afterward.

How many people come each time?

We keep it to 18, enough for a proper mix, small enough that no one gets lost in the room.
